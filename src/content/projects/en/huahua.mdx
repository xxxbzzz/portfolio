## Product Concept

Huahua responds to emotional overload, limited expression, and the fast pace of young people's daily lives. The project uses OH Cards as an entry into subconscious projection, drawing as a container for emotion, and an AI assistant as a gentle companion that asks questions and guides reflection. Feelings that are difficult to name can become visual records that can be viewed, saved, and revisited.

The product is built around "feeling + exploration." It does not offer diagnoses or fixed conclusions. Instead, cards, drawing, and conversation help users slowly approach their current emotional state.

## App Experience

The app is structured around five main entrances: gallery, OH Cards, tests, free drawing, and personal center. The gallery stores past drawing records; the OH Card module allows users to choose a topic or receive AI recommendations; the chat interface asks questions based on the card and previous records; free drawing lets users express their current emotions through color and marks.

The experience is organized around three character strengths: curiosity, courage, and appreciation. Tests and exercises are not treated as one-off questionnaires. They are embedded into repeated daily use, allowing users to gradually form their own growth trajectory.

## Extended Output

Beyond the app prototype, the project extends into a brand website and an AIGC promotional video. The website introduces the brand concept, OH Card theory, and three character-building modes. The video uses cards, lines, and drawing processes to restage the core interaction, communicating the idea of projecting the inner world through OH Cards and guiding emotional flow through lines.

The overall visual system uses warm colors drawn from the cards, light linework, and dotted prompt elements to create an interface that feels approachable, expressive, and lively.
