## Project Setting

The project is set in a future where people have gradually become used to constant-temperature life. Cities, buildings, and transport systems keep temperature within a comfortable range. Natural weather and the body heat of others are slowly filtered through technology and screens. People continue to live efficiently, yet begin to lose sensitivity to the subtle coldness, heat, feverishness, and returning warmth within the body.

"Temperature Consultancy" emerges from this condition. It sits between a body observation station, a resting room, and a healing space. Through recording and guided conversation, consultants help visitors understand the emotions, memories, and relationships behind changes in temperature.

## Characters and Worlds

The project extracts three characters from interviews: a candle person who is overly sensitive to temperature change, a cloud person who constantly shifts with the environment, and a glass person whose temperature responses are delayed. Each character corresponds to a different state of bodily temperature perception and has an independent world of memory.

## Media Composition

The final work consists of an interactive website, three accordion picture books, and a live performance. Viewers first enter a 3D temperature consultation room, then use consultation files on the desk to enter different characters' memory worlds. The picture books unfold bodily experience like case files, while the stage performance makes the role of the "temperature consultant" and the consultation process feel more concrete.
